Computer Science and Engineering
Jashore University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh

My research interests center on how artificial intelligence systems represent information, generalize beyond their training data, and produce reliable outputs. I explore these questions through computer vision, natural language processing, and multimodal learning, with an emphasis on the capabilities and limitations of deep learning and large language models.

My work connects empirical evaluation with the study of model behavior. My ongoing master’s thesis investigates factual reliability and hallucinations in Bengali language models. Earlier research includes real-time vision-based detection and a survey of traditional and multimodal deep learning approaches.

Master’s thesis In progress

BengHalluGen: A Naturalistic Hallucination Taxonomy and Benchmark for Native Bengali Language Models

Working title · M.Sc.Engg. research · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Alam Hossain

Investigating naturally occurring hallucinations in Bengali language models and comparing their factual reliability with multilingual baselines.

  • Developing a benchmark across factual question answering, summarization, and open-ended generation.
  • Exploring a Bengali-focused hallucination taxonomy and a human annotation framework.
  • Planning comparative evaluation of hallucination rates, error types, and inter-annotator agreement.

Real-time Detection of Violent Behavior in Educational Institutions Through Deep Vision-Based Surveillance

Supervisor: Jubayer Al Mahmud · Funded by the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh
